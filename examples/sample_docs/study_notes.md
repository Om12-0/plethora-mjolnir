## Physics (PHY2049)
- RC circuit discharge: V(t) = V0 * exp(-t / RC). The time constant tau
  is the time to fall to ~37% of V0. Completed the capacitor lab report
  in June covering the experiment and the exponential fit.
- Energy stored in a capacitor: E = 0.5 * C * V^2.

## Databases (COP4710)
- Normalization: 1NF (atomic), 2NF (no partial dependency), 3NF (no
  transitive dependency). Redundant data in a restaurant ordering schema
  should be split into customers, orders, order_lines, menu_items.
- Indexes: B-tree for range queries, hash for equality.
